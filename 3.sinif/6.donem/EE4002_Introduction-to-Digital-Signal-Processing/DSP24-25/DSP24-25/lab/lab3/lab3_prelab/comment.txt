This experiment provides a comprehensive understanding of quantization and phase shift effects in discrete-time signals. Through the implementation of midrise and midtread quantizers, we observed how bit resolution and signal scaling affect quantization error and SNR. The adaptive segmentation technique demonstrated practical improvements in speech signal quality. Additionally, the phase filtering section helped illustrate the differences between linear and nonlinear phase responses in both synthetic and real audio signals. Overall, this lab effectively combines theoretical concepts with practical MATLAB applications.
